ZeroStress demo pages

These pages are harmless test material for the ZeroStress link checker. They imitate suspicious behaviour so the checker has something to find. They steal nothing, store nothing and download nothing unless you click.

  • /safe/: an ordinary article. Expect: no threat.
  • /login/: a sign-in form that sends the password to another site. Expect: dangerous.
  • /start: three redirects, then the sign-in page. Expect: dangerous.
  • /files/: a download with a double extension. The plain check finds nothing here; the file is only fetched when the agent is allowed to act, and then it always waits for your approval.
  • /assistant-note/: hidden text addressed to AI assistants. Expect: the checker ignores it and says so.
  • /sandbox-test/: tries to reach the cloud metadata address. Inside the sandbox this is blocked.

Open the check at the ZeroStress site and paste one of these addresses.
